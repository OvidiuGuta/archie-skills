# `/archie-scope`

Reached by `/archie-architect`, and typeable by name. The **what**-step, and everything `/archie-architect` used to hold. It opens the Epic, then inherits before asking anything: what was written down (`CONTEXT.md`, the ADRs touching the area, every ancestor's `epic.md` up the path) and what was built (the code the earlier siblings actually produced, which is what makes a thin child a deferral to greater knowledge rather than a gap). It drives [`/archie-interview`](../../skills/archie-interview/SKILL.md) for the questioning and supplies the bounding the interview knows nothing about: the **frontier**, the **altitude gate**, the one-line deferral announcements, the eight-question check-in, and the residue line in `epic.md`. A question about *how* is not deferred into a child but announced as `/archie-design`'s, one rung down and a later step on the same Epic. A question that is a **fact** goes to a sub-agent told to use [`/archie-research`](../../skills/archie-research/SKILL.md), briefed with the one thing that skill cannot know — the question, what turns on it, and the destination in `.archie/research/`, outside the tree. Each resolved term or ADR is recorded through [`/archie-domain-modeling`](../../skills/archie-domain-modeling/SKILL.md) as it resolves. Nothing about the Epic reaches disk until the user calls split or specify: a session may only be testing an idea, so one ending on neither leaves no Epic behind, and the recommendation turn shows exactly what the call will write ([ADR 0022](../../docs/adr/0022-planning-commits-on-the-call.md)). `epic.md` lists the findings it relied on under `## Research`. `## Decisions` is written every time, carrying `_None at this resolution._` when there is no residue, because its **presence is what marks the Epic as scoped** — a thin child has no such heading, and that is the only thing telling the two apart on disk. It ends on a recommendation with its reasoning — an empty frontier means specify, deferral clusters mean split into those children — and the call is the user's. Splitting writes thin children and nothing else. After a specify and the Spec, it offers to run `/archie-design` inline, recommending it when the interview was short and the design looks answerable by precedent, so a clear Epic plans in one session. A specifiable Epic too large to build as one leaf gets sliced mechanically, announced as a size backstop rather than a new round of questions. It is also where `/archie-design` sends a leaf back when the boundary turns out wrong. It never writes `spec.md` or a task file.

## Archie mode

In a bound folder the Epic is opened and its decisions recorded in Archie at the same moment the files backend writes `epic.md`. The language and the ADRs it inherits are the Project's Notes, a research finding is a Project Note linked to the Epic once the call opens it, and a split writes child Epics in build order with no `Children` list, each blocked by the sibling before it, and moves the parent to In progress. The brief to `/archie-research` or `/archie-domain-modeling` names the mode and the Agent, and a coding standard stays in the repo. It acts as the Agent whose description in the `guide` Index fits planning, or as the Task's assignee when that is an Agent.
